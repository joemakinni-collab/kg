About

The story so far.

Broadcaster · Host · Founder

Loud where it matters.

Kamene Goro is a Kenyan broadcaster, podcast host and brand-builder whose voice has shaped morning radio, primetime conversation and a growing digital community across East Africa.

From the studio to the stage, the work is the same: ask the questions everyone is thinking, hold space for honesty, and make every room feel a little more alive when she walks in.

This site is her front door — a place to shop the merch, book the work, join a private consult or pick up a digital drop.
